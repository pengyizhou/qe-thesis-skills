# Storyline context (your own thesis)

Standing facts about your own report or thesis, read by the coach at Stage 0 and whenever the storyline is in question. This file ships empty: fill it in during the first discussion, or let the coach write down what you decide. The coach updates it only with your agreement, when a stage changes a decision recorded here. Everything in it should be something you stated; a term or framing the coach proposed and you have not confirmed is marked as a proposal.
